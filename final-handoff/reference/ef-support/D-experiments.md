# D. Experience experiments: Hold, Start, Cross

18 Oct 2026. These are three small, isolated interaction concepts, not a redesign. None is approved and none touches the main prototype.

- **Prototype:** https://claude.ai/artifact/L4bbLsjSiL9wosUWxpb9kB. The source is `D-experiments.html` and the screens are in `screens/`.
- **Each step** in the prototype shows what is on screen, the mechanism, the evidence grade, the burden, and what would falsify it.
- **Sample data:** the week of 19–23 October, with volleyball on Wednesday and trash collection on Friday morning.

## 1. Hold: say when, once

Covers mechanisms 1, 2, 3 and 5 from the shortlist.

1. **Capture.** Mark writes one line and picks a kind: has a deadline, hold for a moment, or just an idea. Then he picks when it should come back, from moments the phone can detect:
   - when work ends;
   - when I get home;
   - after a calendar event;
   - this weekend;
   - keep it visible;
   - pick a time, which is offered last.
2. **The moment.** When his Work Focus turns off, one notification arrives with three actions: Later (next time work ends), Start 10 min, and Already done. Swiping it away leaves the item on the widget.
3. **The deadline.** "Trash out" sits on the Lock Screen and Watch from 18:00 to 22:00, and one last call comes at 21:15. "I'm on it" silences that call.
4. **Held.** One short list in three groups: deadlines, held for a moment, and ideas with no pressure. Nothing is ever overdue.

## 2. Start: make the first ten minutes the whole commitment

Covers mechanism 4.

1. **Before starting.** He sees the first step he wrote last time and picks a runway of 5, 10 or 25 minutes.
2. **While it runs.** A Live Activity and the Watch show the time remaining. Stopping is presented as fine.
3. **When the runway ends.** The Watch taps once with three choices: keep going, stop here, or finished.
4. **What gets recorded.** A start and a finish are recorded separately and honestly. Choosing or holding something earns nothing.

## 3. Cross: leave work at work

Covers mechanism 6.

1. **Trigger.** When his Work Focus turns off, a silent offer appears.
2. **Leaving work behind.** He types what's still on his mind. Each line waits until work starts tomorrow.
3. **Tonight.** Only his calendar and his deadlines. On Wednesday volleyball fills the evening, so nothing else is offered. On Tuesday one saved idea appears, with three answers: Tonight, Some other time, Not this one.
4. **Close.** Nothing is scored or counted.

## Simulated, or needing an on-device check

- **Work Focus trigger.** It assumes Meridian's Focus filter runs when the Work Focus turns off. The docs confirm it runs when a Focus engages. Running on turn-off is my reading, and it needs to be tested on a device.
- **Lock Screen and Watch.** These are drawn as mockups, not real widgets. Watch double tap is described in the notes only.
- **Counts.** The "started" and "finished" counts on the last Start screen are illustrative. Whether to show them at all is an open question (F).

## How to test them on Mark

A self-test of 2–3 weeks is the only evidence that matters for one person. Each concept's "Falsified if" line in `B-shortlist.md` is written to be checked by Mark's own account and by simple counts:

- cues answered versus ignored;
- items acted on versus left to fade;
- how many evenings Cross was used.

No questionnaires.
