# Proactive executive-function support (research workstream)

18 Oct 2026. This was a parallel investigation. **Nothing here is approved**, and none of it touches the main prototype.

**Answer in brief.** Meridian could help, but mainly with **remembering** and **starting**, and much less with suggesting.
- The best adult ADHD evidence points at two gaps: turning a vague intention into a plan, and remembering at a clock time. Remembering when an event happens is largely intact.
- So Mark should name the moment as something the phone can detect, get one cue then, and keep the item visible afterwards instead of being re-notified.
- A first step and a ten-minute runway may help with starting.
- Inferring "good moments" isn't worth it.
