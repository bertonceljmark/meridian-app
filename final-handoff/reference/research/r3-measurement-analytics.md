# R3: Measuring feeling and functioning, passive signals, honest personal analytics

Scope: priority 4 (optional depth). Everything here should run quietly underneath routines, never as the product's centre. Contract basis: v1.1.1 (R-09, R-10, R-13, R-14, R-17).

## 1. Established evidence

**Self-report**

1. **Burden comes from how long each check-in is, not mainly from how often prompts arrive.** (Moderate.) A meta-analysis of 496 EMA samples found mean compliance of 79%. The number of prompts did not predict compliance; money did ([Wrzus & Neubauer](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9999286)). In a randomised design, longer questionnaires raised burden and frequency did not ([Eisele et al.](https://cris.maastrichtuniversity.nl/en/publications/the-effects-of-sampling-frequency-and-questionnaire-length-on-per/)). A 2025 reanalysis found that higher frequency increases careless responding ([Ulitzsch et al.](https://cris.maastrichtuniversity.nl/ws/files/262214670/Viechtbauer-2025-Investigating-the-effect-of-experience-sampling.pdf)). → If Meridian ever asks, each ask should take about one gesture. Research compliance is bought with payment, which Meridian doesn't have, so its own compliance will probably be lower.
2. **Momentary mood separates within a person into about three dimensions: valence (pleasant–unpleasant), energetic arousal and calmness (calm–tense).** Two-item scales for each are reliable and sensitive to change ([Wilhelm & Schoebi 2007](https://econtent.hogrefe.com/doi/10.1027/1015-5759.23.4.258), abstract only). (Moderate.) → Of Mark's candidate list, *energy*, *mood* and *tension/overwhelm* are distinct. Stress, irritability and overwhelm probably sit mostly on calm–tense plus valence. Clarity, initiation and concentration are *functioning*, not affect, and suit behavioural anchors better than feeling ratings (see §2).
3. **Single-item energy and fatigue measures hold up in diary studies.** A one-item daily fatigue measure showed strong convergent validity ([van Hooff 2007](https://publications.tno.nl/publication/34619116/6i56Za/hooff-2007-how.pdf)). A *pictorial* single-item energy scale (battery-like images) was validated for experience sampling ([Weigelt et al.](https://research.uni-luebeck.de/en/publications/time-to-recharge-batteriesdevelopment-and-validation-of-a-pictori/)). Single items are inappropriate for ambiguous constructs ([Allen et al. 2022](https://aiedpsych.owlstown.net/publications/33637-single-item-measures-in-psychological-science), via secondary summary). (Moderate.) → A one-tap energy item is defensible. A one-tap "functioning" item is not, because the construct is ambiguous.
4. **Non-numeric affect pickers exist.** The Photographic Affect Meter (choose a photo from a grid; based on the circumplex) was validated against PANAS and the Affect Grid ([Pollak et al. CHI 2011](https://ecommons.cornell.edu/entities/publication/561d1ae3-02b0-404b-8f2b-f8c0656899e2)). Its image set is unpublished ([arXiv 2211.01015](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.01015)). Apple's `HKStateOfMind` (iOS 18) stores valence, labels and associations, as either `dailyMood` or `momentaryEmotion` ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/hkstateofmind)). (Moderate.) → Picking a word or an image avoids the "is it a 6 or a 7?" problem. State of Mind gives Meridian interoperability with the Health app for free.
5. **Recalling yesterday is not the same as sampling moments.** The Day Reconstruction Method reproduced aggregate ESM patterns ([Kahneman 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15576620/)). A direct comparison found high agreement on aggregates but *quite low* agreement on within-person differences ([Lucas et al. 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7508776)). (Moderate.) → An evening reflection is fine for summaries ("weeks like this feel like…"). It is weak for detecting day-to-day associations.
6. **Rating mood rarely harms and may raise awareness. It also slightly changes what is measured.** Reactivity findings are mixed and depend on the protocol ([Eisele 2022 reactivity](https://cris.maastrichtuniversity.nl/en/publications/a-mixed-method-investigation-into-measurement-reactivity-to-the-e)). In one sample 22% said retrospectively that their mood worsened, with no change in the ratings themselves ([Kivelä 2023](https://scholarlypublications.universiteitleiden.nl/access/item%3A3677207/view)). Self-monitoring raised emotional self-awareness, which mediated fewer depressive symptoms ([Kauer 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3414872)). Putting feelings into words dampens their intensity ([Torre & Lieberman 2018](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1754073917742706)). (Moderate.) → Logging how he feels can be a small benefit in itself. Treat it as an experience, not only as data collection.
7. **Missing self-report is not random.** Higher stress and negative affect predicted missing the next prompt (n=260; [Murray et al. 2023](https://www.research.ed.ac.uk/en/publications/prompt-level-predictors-of-compliance-in-an-ecological-momentary-/)). In a larger sensing study, past answering behaviour and location (home or work vs. in transit) dominated and mood mattered little ([Reiter & Schoedel 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11133120)). (Moderate.) → "Days with entries" over-represent calm, at-home days. Any self-report average needs this caveat, and context should be modelled as a cause of missingness.

**Passive signals** (Apple types verified at developer.apple.com)

| Signal | Class | Evidence as a well-being indicator |
|---|---|---|
| Workouts, steps, exercise minutes, `timeInDaylight` (iOS 17) | Direct behaviour | Good for "what happened". Daylight: brighter days and darker nights are associated with lower psychiatric risk, observational ([Burns 2023, Monash summary](https://www.monash.edu/news/articles/largest-ever-study-on-light-exposure-proves-its-impact-on-mental-health)) |
| Sleep timing and duration (`sleepAnalysis`) | Reliable physiology (sleep vs. wake); stages weaker | Sleep/wake sensitivity ≥95%; stage sensitivity 50–86%; Apple Watch underestimates deep sleep by about 43 min ([PSG study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/pmc11511193), snippet) |
| Resting HR, HRV SDNN (RMSSD new in iOS 27), `appleSleepingWristTemperature` | Reliable physiology; **indirect proxy** for state | Large deviations from personal baseline flag illness ([Mishra 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41551-020-00640-6)). Alcohol dose-dependently suppresses overnight recovery (within-person; [Pietilä 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5878366)). Wearable HRV vs. *perceived stress* coupling is inconclusive ([Martinez 2022](https://humanfactors.jmir.org/2022/3/e33754/)) |
| State of Mind, mindful minutes, `HKScoredAssessment` (PHQ/GAD, iOS 18) | Self-report | As valid as the report itself |
| Phone-sensing composites (location entropy, typing, call logs) | Speculative digital biomarker; largely **inaccessible** on iOS | Studies are small, reporting is poor, "a starting point only" ([De Angel 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8752685/)) |

8. **Logged device use beats recalled use, and its link to well-being is small.** Self-reported media use correlates only moderately with logs ([Parry 2021](https://research.vu.nl/en/publications/a-systematic-review-and-meta-analysis-of-discrepancies-between-lo/)). Technology use explains at most 0.4% of adolescent well-being ([Orben & Przybylski 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6512056)). (Strong.) → If Screen Time is used, it must be logged rather than self-reported, and framed as context, not a verdict.

**9. Screen Time: what Apple currently documents** (all from developer.apple.com, fetched 18 Oct 2026)

- **The API exists.** [`DeviceActivityData.activityData(filteredBy:using:)`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivitydata/activitydata(filteredby:using:)) is **iOS/iPadOS 26.4+**. It returns an `AsyncSequence<DeviceActivityData>` with a `.cached` or `.live` [policy](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivitydata/policy). Apple says: "Use this method to export family activity data, for use in another app or platform." So the data reaches the app's own code and analytics, not only the sandboxed report extension. **E-11 is outdated.**
- **Authorization.** It needs [`approvedWithDataAccess`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/familycontrols/authorizationstatus/approvedwithdataaccess) (26.4+), on top of `requestAuthorization(for: .individual)` (Face ID self-authorization; [docs](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/familycontrols/authorizationcenter/requestauthorization(for:))). It also needs the [`com.apple.developer.family-controls.app-and-website-usage`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/entitlements/com.apple.developer.family-controls.app-and-website-usage) entitlement in addition to `family-controls`. It unlocks real bundle IDs, domains and category names.
- **Region.** "You may develop and test... on devices in all regions by using an Apple-provided provisioning profile. Customer installations... only... on devices located in the EU... signed in with an Apple Account with an EU country or region." Slovenia is in the EU, so Mark qualifies either way. **A development-signed Xcode install on his own iPhone is explicitly the supported test path.** A paid account is needed for the capability; free Personal Team support is unverified.
- **Exclusivity.** This is documented: "Only one app at a time can hold this authorization status on a given device." **Unverified but serious:** a developer reports that Apple's consent sheet warns that Screen Time "will lose access to this data", and that the Settings Screen Time pane stopped updating after granting access ([forum 844661](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/844661), Sep 2026, no Apple reply). If true, Mark would trade Apple's own Screen Time view for Meridian's.
- **Granularity.** Segments can be [hourly, daily or weekly](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivityfilter/segmentinterval-swift.enum). Each [`ActivitySegment`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivitydata/activitysegment) has total duration, `longestActivity` (a DateInterval), `firstPickup`, pickups without app use, and per-category and per-app duration, pickups and notifications ([ApplicationActivity](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivitydata/applicationactivity)). There are no per-session start/stop events. **The hour is the finest documented resolution.**
- **Multiple devices.** `Device.Model` covers iPhone, iPad, iPod and mac; devices share data only if the person enables it in iCloud ([Devices](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivityfilter/devices-swift.struct)). Whether Mac data reaches an iPhone app under individual authorization is **unverified**.
- **Reliability.** Errors are `unavailable`, `unauthorized` and `missingData` ([Error](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivitydata/error)). Forums report threshold and latency bugs in iOS 26.x ([tag index](https://developer.apple.com/forums/tags/device-activity)). App Store distribution of the usage tier is murky ([844623](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/844623)), but that is irrelevant to a personal install.
- **Combining with sleep.** Hourly segments can be intersected with `sleepAnalysis` intervals. Usage inside an hour that is partly asleep can only be *bounded* (for example, "0–35 min possibly asleep"), not located. `longestActivity` sometimes narrows it. **Background playback:** whether screen-off YouTube audio counts as usage is undocumented. Screen-on video during sleep probably counts, consistent with Mark's report (inference).

**Analytics**

10. **Group findings don't transfer to one person.** Within-person variance was up to four times the between-person variance ([Fisher et al. PNAS 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6142277)). (Strong.) → Meridian should not import population claims as personal ones. Its personal estimates need their own uncertainty.
11. **One person's data supports only a few relationships.** With 75–100 time points, sensitivity of idiographic temporal models is low, so networks should be cut to about 6 variables ([Psych Methods 2023](https://ifp.nyu.edu/2023/journal-article-abstracts/met0000466/)). (Moderate.) → Candidate relationships must be few and pre-specified, not mined.
12. **Observational n-of-1 data can generate hypotheses. Testing them needs a design.** ([Daza 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6087468)) (Moderate.)
13. **Stating uncertainty barely costs trust.** Numeric uncertainty lowers confidence in the number, not in the source ([van der Bles 2020](https://research.rug.nl/en/publications/the-effects-of-communicating-uncertainty-on-public-trust-in-facts/)). (Moderate.) → Honest ranges are not an engagement cost.
14. **Consumer products are opaque.** Exist needs 3 weeks, refreshes weekly, shows strength plus a 5-star "confidence", and documents no method or multiplicity control ([KB](https://kb.exist.io/article/18-how-long-does-it-take-to-find-correlations), [KB](https://kb.exist.io/article/37-what-are-correlations)). Bearable needs 3 days with and 3 days without a factor, and admits confounding ([support](https://bearable.app/support/troubleshooting/my-correlations-are-wrong-dont-make-sense/)). Oura's Readiness score has undisclosed weights against 14-day and 2-month baselines ([Oura](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/360057791533)). → These are the patterns R-17 forbids: all-pairs mining on tiny samples and composite scores.
15. **For reduction goals, totals recalled at the end of a period are adequate. Day-by-day recall is not.** Calendar-recall (timeline followback) agreement with daily reports is poor day by day but "adequate for capturing aggregate measures". Underestimation grows with longer recall windows and heavier drinking ([Morgenstern et al. 2014 review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4432849)). (Moderate.) → One weekly confirmation is enough evidence for "at most N this week"; daily confirmations are unnecessary.

## 2. Reasonable hypotheses (inferences)

- **Behavioural anchors measure functioning better than ratings.** Concrete, checkable questions like "Did you start the thing you'd been avoiding?", "Lost an hour to drift?" or "Finished what you sat down for?" turn clarity, initiation and concentration into observations with near-zero ambiguity. This suits a perfectionist because there is a factually right answer.
- **Coarse, relative judgments ("lower / usual / higher than my normal") plus "can't say" will beat 1–10 for Mark.** There are fewer decision points and nothing to calibrate (untested here).
- **Simple arithmetic on data needs (inference).** Detecting a same-person correlation of r = .30 at 80% power needs about 85 independent days. Typical day-to-day autocorrelation inflates that to roughly 140 or more. Most personal relationships will therefore stay "not yet knowable" for months, and the product must make that state feel normal and honest.
- **Defensible analysis method:** pre-register a small menu of plausible pairs with fixed lags. Examples: alcohol → that night's resting HR and HRV; late caffeine → sleep onset; daylight or exercise → next-day energy; sleep → next-day energy. Use within-person centring, a time trend and an AR(1) term, and adjust for context spans (work, travel, sick). Use Benjamini–Hochberg across the menu, report effect sizes with intervals, and do sensitivity checks excluding imputed or low-confidence days. Data-driven candidates appear only as "worth watching", never as findings.
- **Context is the main confounder and the main cause of missing data** (finding 7). Context spans are therefore an analysis prerequisite, not decoration.

## 3. Speculative opportunities

- **Feeling as a gesture, not a form.** A single two-axis touch (pleasant ↔ unpleasant × drained ↔ charged) on the Watch writes `HKStateOfMind` (valence) plus Meridian's energy. Optional words come after. The physical feel matters more than the numbers.
- **Measurement bursts instead of perpetual check-ins** ([burst designs](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/measurement-burst-design/)). Mark opts into a "sampling week" two or three times a year, or when starting or changing a routine. Questions are dense for a few days, then completely silent. This gives better data than sparse daily nagging, and it ends.
- **Event-anchored micro-reports.** After a workout, a date, an evening without alcohol or a long focus session, offer one optional tap: "how's your energy now?" Associations then come attached to the moment they concern, and the input feels like part of the routine's satisfying close rather than a chore.
- **Physiology as gentle context, not scores.** "Overnight heart rate well above your usual" next to an alcohol or illness observation. No readiness number, and no HRV-as-stress claim.
- **Weekly period close for reduction goals.** On the last day of a reduction week: "2 logged. Is that everything?" with **Complete / Not sure**. Unanswered weeks stay "2 logged, unconfirmed": shown neutrally, never as failure or success. A "that's all for tonight" option at logging time also closes the evening. HealthKit `numberOfAlcoholicBeverages` from other apps can be merged.
- **"Knowability" as a visible, rewarding meter.** A non-score signal per candidate relationship, such as "needs about 6 more weeks of comparable weekdays". It turns waiting into anticipation without inventing findings.
- **Personal behaviour experiments, never medical or substance ones (R-22).** For example: alternate weeks with and without a morning daylight walk, then look at energy. This turns a suggestive pattern into a fair comparison.
- **Screen Time (if Mark accepts the exclusivity cost):** show three honest bands: raw usage, usage bounded inside sleep (a range), and estimated awake usage. Pickups and `firstPickup` relative to wake time are interesting and non-judgemental.

## 4. Challenges to current assumptions

- **"Screen Time access is impossible" is wrong** for an EU or development-signed personal install on iOS 26.4+. But **the integration may switch off Apple's own Screen Time pane**, which is a real product cost Mark should decide on.
- **Exact awake vs. asleep usage separation is impossible.** Hourly segments allow only bounded estimates (R-14's "qualified" estimates are the right framing).
- **"Functioning" is not one thing,** and some candidate dimensions overlap (stress, overwhelm and irritability). Tracking all eight would be redundant burden.
- **Evening recall isn't a cheap substitute for in-the-moment reporting** for within-day associations (finding 5).
- **Missing data is informative.** R-09's exclusion of unknowns is necessary but not enough: averages over *recorded* days are biased toward good days.
- **The discovery reward will be scarce** with honest thresholds. If priority 4 promises frequent insights, it will either disappoint or cheat.
