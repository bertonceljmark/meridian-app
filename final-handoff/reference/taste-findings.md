# Taste check: what Mark's ratings say (20 Oct 2026)

Source: 50 blind ratings in the taste-check artifact db (FfYWfnsp3EAAREH7NEny1u), rated 11:00 to 11:12. No extras added. Mark's message 11:14: loves (Not Boring) apps, worried the style is too minimal to fit everything.

## Results
- **Real apps, 11 of 24 love:** Oura, Sleep Cycle, Endel, Opal, Halide, Gentler Streak, Bevel, Tiimo, Partiful, NB Habits (3D summit), NB Weather (3D precip). **No:** Things 3, Streaks, Duolingo. Meh: WHOOP, Flighty, Structured, Carrot, Clash Royale, Chess.com, Strava, Headspace, How We Feel, Finch ("childish").
- **Our ten flat styles, 0 of 10 love:** meh for Swiss (Detail), Game (childish), Playful (childish) and Generative; no for the other six. His note on Soft calm: "I dont like the list layouts".
- **Past Meridian, 2 of 8 love:** Home test 3 "Set as type" and the 18 Oct whole-product prototype. Studio run report got meh. No for Studio today, Refined, Barometer, Perennials and craft study S1-B.
- **Feel:** love for Liquid fill and Mechanical key (his note: "Sure, but not sure about the styling of other sections that could use this"). "Too little" on Springy, Burst, Gentle and Rolling counter. "Too much" on Ink stamp. Instant got meh.

## What it says (inference, from one sitting)
1. **Depth and material, not flat UI.** Nearly every app he loved has a rendered, lit, atmospheric hero: 3D objects (Not Boring, Opal's stones), photographic or volumetric light (Oura, Sleep Cycle, Halide, Endel), or an illustrated scene (Gentler). The flat ones lost, whether calm (Things), loud (Streaks, Duolingo) or ours. All ten of our styles were flat CSS, which most likely explains 0/10. Palette isn't the common thread: he loved dark and light (Bevel, Tiimo) and even maximal (Partiful).
2. **One big hero number or object, with detail underneath.** Oura 88, Sleep Cycle dial, Opal timer, Whoop-like rings were meh, but hero plus atmosphere wins.
3. **Motion needs weight and continuity.** Quick springs and confetti are "too little", a slam is "too much". He loved material that physically responds: a key that travels and latches, and liquid that fills. This matches Not Boring's "layer and push".
4. **Structure was not the failure.** The two past designs that hold the most function (Set as type, whole-product) are the two he loved. What he rejected were their surfaces: paper, editorial serif, flat lists.
5. **Not cute.** Childish was tagged three times. A character can work in a scene (Gentler) but not as a mascot UI.

## On his worry: is Not Boring too minimal to hold Meridian?
Not Boring is minimal in scope, not in design (Allen: "Minimalism is a fun place to visit… not somewhere I want to live", see integration/research/not-boring.md). NB Weather already carries dense data: hourly charts, 10-day, AQI, UV, a moon table and widgets, under one 3D hero. The parts he loves (material, light, depth, heavy feedback, sound, haptics) are a surface and feel layer. They can sit on top of an information structure that already holds everything, and he loved two of those. Things that don't transfer: one habit per screen, spectacle on every tap (tier it), rewarding observations.

## Recommended next experiment
One small test: the Set as type home plus one report and the log tap, given a dimensional, lit material with mechanical or liquid feedback, driven against the hard set (review/CAPABILITY-CHECK.md). Not a Not Boring reskin: own material, own forms. It answers whether "loved structure + loved surface" is something he loves, and whether it stays calm at 25 items.
