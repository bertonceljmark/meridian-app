# E. Interaction exploration

18 Oct 2026. Prototype: https://claude.ai/artifact/18qy1PiyQwjWe3B8exqgV4 (source `prototype/patterns-lab.html`). It is a research instrument for this workstream, **not** a proposed visual direction for Meridian. Workstream A owns the identity. Nothing in the main prototype was changed.

## What the ten steps demonstrate

| Step | Interaction | The point |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Create | Name, plus chips for kind, shape, use and recording practice. A live list of what it can be compared with and what devices can't know | Descriptors are chosen through plain words, never statistics. The engine's output is visible at creation, so nothing about analysis is hidden |
| 2 Log | One tap per item, a toast with undo. Headache onset or end as optional afterthoughts | Recording stays inside the existing logging grammar (whole/A-architecture §3). Nothing to rate |
| 3 Note | One line, saved. Two days later the "same thing" chip links a second note | Context without a journal. No category, severity, end date or follow-up |
| 4 Time | Lanes for notes, stated/imported context, possible circumstances, records and sleep. Unworn nights shown as "?" | Facts and possibilities look different. Unknown is drawn as unknown |
| 5 Patterns | A time-ordered map (evening → night → next day) of every generated comparison, coloured by evidence state, then grouped lists. "Pretend I never wrote notes" switch | Shows the system's scope and its honesty at a glance, and that robustness doesn't depend on notes |
| 6 Detail: caffeine and the car | Dot strip of every comparable night; four context assumptions animate which dots are set aside and where the interval lands; week-influence bars with note flags; checks list | Uncertainty and context made tangible, without statistical vocabulary |
| 7 Melatonin | "The night before" vs "that night" intervals | Selection explained in one picture, and an explicit "what this can't say" |
| 8 Headache | One column per recorded headache vs its own matched earlier nights | Event-only analysis that never assumes headache-free days |
| 9 Sauna | Overlap with volleyball stated plainly | A new item gets the same scrutiny on day one |
| 10 Explore | Pick any input, outcome and lag | Deliberate exploration with the same honesty, labelled "you looked for this" and excluded from findings |

## Interaction principles this suggests

1. **Analytics needs nothing from Mark beyond normal logging.** The descriptor comes from creating the item, and coverage from how he says he'll record it. A note is optional and a single line.
2. **Dots before numbers.** Every result opens to its raw comparison. The "too small to matter" band does more for understanding than a p-value.
3. **States are the interface.** Building, Changes with context, Already different beforehand and the rest carry the meaning. Numbers are secondary.
4. **Context is shown as a "what if" switch, never as an edit.** The four assumptions are a segmented control. Nothing is deleted or marked invalid.
5. **Patterns is somewhere to go, not something that comes to you.** No cards are pushed.
6. **Generic charts only where generic is right.** Workout and sleep reports stay bespoke. The analytics layer would add a small "related" strip to them, linking to the comparison.

## What this prototype does not settle

- The visual language (Workstream A).
- Where Patterns lives in navigation. The architecture places it inside Time, and this prototype doesn't change that.
- How the relationship map scales beyond about 40 comparisons. At larger sizes it should probably filter by one chosen outcome.
- Whether the four-way assumption control is understandable without explanation. It needs a test with Mark.
