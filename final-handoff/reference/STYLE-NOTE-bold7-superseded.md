# Meridian Bold 7: one rendered object per screen, on a deep stage (anchor: Opal, NB Weather)
- Opal, observed: a single lit, gem-cut object sits on an even, near-black ground with a few very large, thin numerals and one line of type. Colour lives only in the object; panels are neutral dark plates with hairline edges, the main button is a plain white pill, and the dark tab bar stays quiet. NB Weather, observed: a 3D scene fills the top of each screen with a large rounded bottom edge. The object is sized by the data and answers drag and tilt, while the hourly and 10-day data sit below in plain cards. The scene sinks as you scroll.
- The move, on the micro base: every screen opens on a deep stage (#0B1019, deep in light and dark alike) with a 38 px rounded bottom. The stage holds that screen's three.js object (0.160.0, importmap), built from the screen's data: Today and the run report show the run as a tube; sleep shows night blocks; Water is a tumbler (full is the aim, it never runs over); every other routine page shows its period as cut stones (observations as matte, unlit stones). Time is twelve banded stones, each month and day a platter of blocks, Routines a column per daily routine, You four module stones, a session report and Workouts columns, and onboarding stones. Inside a stage, the tokens remap so text reads light on deep.
- Redrawn components: thin 280–360 weight figures and quiet 15 px section labels. Cards, rows, tiles, Now rows, plates, the timeline, chips and presets each sit on their own plate made of one material (hairline edge, top light, short contact shadow). Lists are separate plates. Tokens and Settled discs are jewels set in deep ink. The tab bar is deep in both themes and the log key is a lit white disc. Toasts, the live bar and the scrubber use the stage ink. Every control you press is a key of deep ink with a bevel, and what it selects lights as a jewel or a white key. Sheets, toasts, the hold scrubber, chart panels, the Lock Screen, widget and Watch previews, every page title block, onboarding and the week story are all stages.
- Motion: objects assemble on entry, turn by drag with inertia, lean on press and turn slowly when idle. Scrolling turns and sinks each object, and the stage's title gives way (CSS scroll timeline). The run keeps its angle from Today into the report. Logging springs the water and lights stones. All micro interactions are kept, and reduced motion holds still.
- Performance: one renderer per hero at DPR ≤ 2. It draws only while on screen, not on a page pushed underneath, and not in a hidden tab, and is fully disposed on unmount (after a 25-step journey, one canvas remains). A flat SVG fallback covers no WebGL. Medication has a stage but no object: it is a record, not a reward.
- Checks: light, dark, 135% and reduced motion; 0 console errors. Babel blocks 418/83/47/191 KB, and the module sits outside Babel (33 KB).
